Our Story

The Gaming Store started with a simple frustration: finding good gaming gear in South Africa meant endless shipping delays and prices that made no sense. So we built a store that stocks the stuff players actually want — controllers, headsets, wheels and mobile gear — at prices in rands, shipped locally.

What We Stock

  • Controllers — DualSense, Access Controller and third-party pads.
  • Audio — headsets built for long sessions and late-night raids.
  • Racing — wheels and pedals for sim racers.
  • Mobile — clips and controllers that turn your phone into a handheld.

Why Shop With Us

  • Local stock, no import surprises.
  • Prices listed in rands, with no hidden fees.
  • Real people answering your questions.
