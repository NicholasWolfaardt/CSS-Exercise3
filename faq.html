Orders & Shipping

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 12:00 on a business day are dispatched the same day. Main centres usually arrive in 1–3 business days, and outlying areas in 3–5. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your parcel leaves us.

How much does shipping cost?

Delivery is a flat R 99 nationwide, and free on orders over R 1 500. We ship with a courier to your door — no PO Box deliveries.

Can I collect my order instead?

Yes. Choose collection at checkout and we'll email you when it's ready, usually within a few hours during trading hours. Bring your order number and ID.

Do you ship outside South Africa?

Not yet — we currently deliver within South Africa only. Cross-border shipping is on the list for the future.

Products & Stock

Are your products genuine and under warranty?

All stock is sourced from official distributors and carries the manufacturer's warranty — typically 12 months, longer on some items. Keep your invoice; it's your proof of purchase.

What if an item is out of stock?

Message us and we'll give you an ETA or suggest the closest alternative. Popular items like controllers restock weekly.

Do the controllers work on PC?

The DualSense and Access Controller work on PC via USB or Bluetooth, though some features are PS5-only. The Logitech wheel works on PC, PS4 and Xbox depending on the model — check the product page before ordering.

Returns & Payments

What is your return policy?

Unopened items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened items are accepted within 7 days if they're unused and in original packaging. Faulty items are covered by the manufacturer's warranty.

How do I start a return?

Email us your order number and a photo of the item, and we'll send you a return authorisation and collection details. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of us receiving the item.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Card payments (Visa and Mastercard), instant EFT, and cash on collection. We don't offer cash on delivery.

Is my payment information safe?

We never see or store your card details — payments are handled by our payment provider's secure checkout.

Still stuck?

If your question isn't here, send us a message and we'll get back to you within one business day.
